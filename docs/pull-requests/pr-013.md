Above an answer stands a line that says how many tools the model stopped to ask for on the way to it. Nothing anywhere said which ones. This says them.

**The model.** `thought` keeps every call whole -- the tool it asked, what it asked of it, and what it was given back where anything came back -- and counts as many calls as it keeps, so that a count and the calls it counts cannot disagree (`src/core.cc`). Nothing that kept the number as a number of its own is left: the provider writes the calls out of the stream that asked for them, and a loop that asked twice keeps both rounds' beside each other.

**The screen.** `history_fold`, pressed open, says one line of each under what the model wrote to itself: greentext above, then the calls it counted, in the order it asked them, dim and monospace like the greentext. A call nothing was given back with says nothing of what it was given -- nothing here runs a tool yet. The lines a fold keeps are kept beside it and said again for the next answer that wanted fewer, rather than made again every frame.

**Said once.** How long a thought took, and what one call says of itself, moved into `logic::formatting` (`took_of`, `call_of`): both were going to be said in more than one place, and one of them was about to be. Both are tested (`took_of`, `call_of`), and the two provider tests that counted calls now say which call was counted.

The design and TODO say what is now said and what is not yet: the design's *What the fold keeps today* names the calls, and the bullet that said the fold's thinking and calls are read nowhere yet is written again for what it reads now.
