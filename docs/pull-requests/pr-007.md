The three dots' fourth row: Compact context. It stands grey where there is nothing to keep back -- a conversation of nothing but questions, or one asked already -- and asking twice keeps nothing more back than asking once did.

What is taken away is only what a wire is given: every answer but the last wrote to itself before it answered, and all of that was being asked for again and again, forty times over in a conversation of forty. The last answer keeps its own thinking given, because what it thought is what is being answered about. What the reader can open above an answer loses nothing: the fold says its thinking whole either way, so a compacted conversation is not one anybody can tell was compacted, except that answering it is cheaper.

src/logic/compacted.cc is new, and tested from four directions: read on its own, what is given away and what is not, and the three ways there is nothing to compact.

A press of a row nothing answers yet asks nobody anything now (ask_box), which is what a grey row stood asking for before.

Carries the three dots from #4 and the thinking rows from #1, because the row is drawn in their menu.
