`tools/probe_fold.mjs`, asked of the deployed page the way the probe workflow takes a script by hand: it reads the room through the mirror's own words rather than a place somebody guessed -- the fold's line where it stands, the thought behind it shut, the thought once the pill has been pressed, and the line whole again once it is shut back. It then walks the pill in both directions, saying for every place whether the fold answered, and ends with the press a reader would give on the words themselves.

What it says of today's page: all four reads hold -- the line reads, the thought stands shut, a press at the pill's edge opens it, a press closes it again -- and where a press lands is the finding: the line's own words answer nothing, and the pill answers only in a thumb's width at its left. That is a fault rather than a habit -- the scene sized a text by the children it does not have -- and #14 puts it right: over that tree the walk flips at every step from the pill's left edge to its right and the press on the words opens the fold (https://github.com/progressive-chat/progressive-chat/actions/runs/42372734963). The line `words by mouse opened` in the log says which side of that fix any page stood on.

The TODO item goes with it: what a model wrote to itself has been pressed open and read, and what was found where it landed is written down.

Asked by hand against the deployment -- `gh workflow run probe.yml -f script="$(cat tools/probe_fold.mjs)"` -- and against a ref's own build through `PCHAT_PAGE`, which the script takes where the workflow offers it.
