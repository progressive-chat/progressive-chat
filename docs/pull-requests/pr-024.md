the actual mechanism

`anchoredBox` in skiff computes the box as *anchor point of the parent* **plus** the x/y you give: for `kCentreLeft` that means the centre of the whole settings column (about 1450px) got added to the row's own y, the words landed around 4245 -- past the column's 2900 -- and were culled with everything drawn outside it. Every bare label that draws on this page (the group headings) anchors `kTopLeft`, where the anchor term is zero and only the row's y counts.

what this says

The archive's label now stands by the heading's rule: top-left anchor, the same x, and a y in the middle of its own row. Press and file were already right on the deployed page (`progressive-chat.prgrssv` leaves on the first press); this is the words on the glass.

not touched
