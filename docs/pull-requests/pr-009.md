What the plus at the editor's left is for: it says **Camera**, **Gallery**, **Videos** and **Files** (design-doc.md, "The editor's row"), and each press asks the machine this is running on for one of them.

* **src/platform/chosen.cc** — the asking, as a platform's own: nothing chooses anything yet, and it says so. **src/platform/chosen_web.cc** takes its place wherever this is built for the page, which is the one that asks, through the input a page puts in its own document. Nothing above either of them knows which one answered.
* **src/core.cc** — `attachment`: a name, a kind, and the bytes whole, kept with the message it was said with. What four things are there to choose between is said there too, and not by any platform.
* **src/logic/composer.cc** — what was chosen goes into the one message sent with it, and enough on its own to send without a word written beside it (which is what `can_send` already stood there for).
* **src/ui/composer.cc** — the plus, its menu, and one line above the card naming everything waiting to be sent. Where no machine chooses anything, no plus is drawn to press.
* **src/ui/message_list.cc** — what a message was said with is named under it in the room.
* **src/provider.cc** — what a wire is given: a file that is words is given inside the turn, after the words written beside it; a picture and a moving picture are kept and given to nothing yet, for giving one is its own asking and no wire here answers it.

Tested in **test/provider_test.cc** and **test/logic_test.cc**.

Like the earlier pull requests here, this one says what its own commits say and carries theirs too: every branch in this fork starts from `main`, because nothing can be stacked across forks.
