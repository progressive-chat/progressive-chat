_(exported from the pull request body; see pr-010.json for the file list and pr-010.diff for the diff_)

What a fold says of the calls an answer made is a count: *Thought for 4.2 s · 3
tool calls*. Pressed open it says what the model wrote to itself, and until now
said nothing else — nothing anywhere kept **what each of them asked for or what
it was given back**, though this program answers every call itself
(`tools::owned::answered`, pchat.tools) and could say both.

So each one is kept whole now, with the answer that asked for it:

- `called` in pchat.core: what it is called, what it asked for, what it was
  given back. Standing last in `message`, as everything added after a struct
  was first said does, so what says a message field after field — the sample —
  keeps saying what it said.
- Recorded in `src/app/screens.cc` where the loop answers a call: one `called`
  per round, kept on the answer's own record and written into the message when
  the answer is. Nothing is said twice where a second round asks again.
- Drawn in `src/ui/message_list.cc` as `called_row` — a name line, what it
  asked for, what came back, monospace for both the asking and the answer —
  six at a time under the greentext, and only while the fold is open, since
  what is folded says the count and nothing more.
- Three of the sample's answers keep calls of their own: three for the first
  (one of each tool this program has), one for the second, two for the sieve.
  A fold has something in it to press open before any wire has answered.

The design says this of them (design-doc.md, "Folded above it"), and says it
now of what each was asked and given as well as that there were any.

#3, which said their names and nothing more, was closed in favour of this one — its body said so itself.
