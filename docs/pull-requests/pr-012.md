Settings named **Memory & Data** and drew nothing under it, because what it names is nowhere else in this program: nothing written into a file, nothing remembered between runs -- and nothing that is nowhere may be drawn either.

What Memory says now is what the window is holding, and a press on either row taking it away:

* everything said in the conversation that is open, with how much there is of it, leaving the conversation named in the drawer and open in the room with nothing under it;
* every conversation there is, with how many, leaving nothing open at all.

Both are answered by tests in `test/logic_test.cc` (`pchat.logic.forgotten`), and what a press does is done between frames as every other asking is. Nothing of it is asked back afterwards: this is a forgetting, not a hiding.

The design says of the group now too -- what is remembered of it today, and that what is taken away is not kept anywhere to be asked for.
